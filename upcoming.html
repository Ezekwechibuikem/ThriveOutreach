Thrive Together

Thrive Together is a quarterly fitness and wellness programme by THRIVERIGHT Health Foundation that brings together people living with sickle cell disease and their caregivers.

We want to create a space where warriors can enjoy light, suitable movement activities, have conversations, learn from health professionals, share experiences, and connect with people who understand what they're going through.

Our hope is to build a community where warriors and caregivers feel more supported, and encouraged to keep thriving.

Sickle Cell Crisis: What to Do and What Not to Do

DateMay 9, 2026
Time6:00 PM – 7:00 PM (WAT)
VenueOnline (X Space)

This live X (Twitter) Space brought together medical professionals to discuss sickle cell crises, share practical guidance on responding to emergencies, and address common misconceptions that can put lives at risk. The conversation encouraged people living with sickle cell disease, caregivers and the wider public to learn, ask questions and make informed health decisions.
