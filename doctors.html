MEDICAL & ADVOCACY

Dr. Beckley Emmanuel

Medical Doctor | Sickle Cell Warrior | Advocate | Health Educator

Dr. Beckley Emmanuel is a passionate medical doctor, health advocate, and a proud Sickle Cell Warrior with over five years of professional experience in clinical care, health education, and advocacy. Living with Sickle Cell Disease himself, Dr. Emmanuel combines his medical expertise and personal journey to champion awareness, education, and empowerment for individuals living with Sickle Cell Disease.

Throughout his career, he has worked closely with various Sickle Cell foundations and organizations, leading initiatives that focus on sensitization, early intervention, and improving the quality of life for warriors across communities. His unique perspective as both a healthcare professional and a person living with Sickle Cell Disease positions him as a powerful voice in bridging the gap between clinical knowledge and real-life experiences.

Dr. Emmanuel is deeply committed to mentoring young people, inspiring hope, and equipping students with the right information and strategies to navigate life with Sickle Cell Disease. Through his advocacy, he continues to foster greater understanding, reduce stigma, and promote a supportive environment for all Sickle Cell Warriors.

PUBLIC HEALTH & INNOVATION

Dr. Uchechukwu Alfred Ifedigbo

MBBS, MBA | Public Health Specialist | HIV/TB Project Lead | Digital Health Advocate

Dr. Uchechukwu Alfred Ifedigbo is a seasoned public health professional with over seven years of experience leading and delivering impactful clinical and strategic initiatives within HIV/TB programmes. His career spans both frontline clinical service and high-level programme management, where he has driven measurable improvements in patient outcomes and system efficiency.

Driven by a passion for helping people live longer, healthier lives, Dr. Ifedigbo champions preventative health strategies that integrate clinical expertise with cutting-edge digital health technologies, particularly those powered by AI. His solution-driven mindset and results-oriented approach have earned recognition for innovative problem-solving and scalable interventions in resource-limited settings.

With a solid academic foundation in medicine (MBBS) and business administration (MBA), he brings a unique perspective that combines clinical acumen with strategic leadership, making him a transformative force in the future of global health.
